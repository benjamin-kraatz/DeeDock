Harbor opens on its own a moment after the page loads. Hover a window for its close button, click one to bring it forward, type to filter, use the arrow keys and Return, or click an app in the dock to show only its group. Escape or a click on the wallpaper closes Harbor. To reopen it, press Space or click the Harbor tile next to the Trash. TextEdit’s quote has unsaved changes, so closing it shows how Harbor gets out of the way of the save sheet.
