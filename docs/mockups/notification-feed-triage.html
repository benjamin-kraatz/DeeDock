Notification Feed triage (DEE-155). The popover is live: hover a row for ✕, right-click for the menu, click a row to open its app (the entry leaves, the feed closes as today), drag a row to the left to reveal Clear or swipe it away, click a stack to unfold it. Click the bell tile to close and reopen the feed. The stage takes the keyboard once you click it: ↑↓ select, →← unfold/fold a stack, ↩ open, ⌫ remove, ⌘C copy, ⌘⌫ clear all, esc close. Blue chips mark the recommended default of each variant.

A1 · Opened app clears its entries

  • Opening from the feed: the row drops toward the dock and fades while the app’s tile bounces, then the popover closes as it does today. Variant “Shrinks” keeps today’s scale .92 + fade.
  • Opened elsewhere while the feed is open: same leaving animation in place, the other rows spring up. While closed: the badge simply shrinks.
  • Grouped: opening an app takes the whole stack.

A2 · Clear when closed

  • Off by default. On: everything that was on screen while the feed was open is gone the moment it closes. Arrivals while closed stay and show the unread dot on reopen.
  • The empty state is today’s (bell.slash, “No Notifications Yet”).

B1 · Stacks

  • One row per app, newest in front, count capsule beside the sender, cards peeking 4 pt below, 8 pt narrower each. Single entries are plain rows.
  • Click unfolds in place with a small header (sender, count, Show Less, ✕). Rows slide out from under the front card; Show Less slides them back.
  • ✕ on the stack or the header folds the rows into the front card, then the card fades.
  • Accordion closes other stacks when one unfolds.

B2 · Swipe to clear

  • Dragging left reveals the red action from the trailing edge. Short drag snaps open at 72 pt and leaves the button (click it to clear). Past the threshold or a quick flick commits: the row flies out, the rest spring up.
  • Dragging right rubber-bands. On a stack the action reads “Clear All”.
  • Reduce Motion: the drag still follows the pointer; every removal, arrival and unfold is a 150 ms cross-fade, and the bell does not swing.
