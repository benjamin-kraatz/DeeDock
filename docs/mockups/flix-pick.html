Flix Pick (DEE-164). When an app shows an Open or Save picker, DOKK puts the files that app most likely wants right beside it. Click a row to select it in the picker, ⌥-click or double-click to also press Open, or drag it in as you do today. Drag the picker by its toolbar and the panel follows. The timings are from the two probes on macOS 27.

What the probes proved

  • Pickers carry open-panel / save-panel, sandboxed apps included. They are detected about 40 ms after they open.
  • Clicking a non-activating panel keeps the app in front and its picker focused.
  • A file already listed: select its row through Accessibility, about 70 ms, nothing on screen.
  • Anywhere else: ⇧⌘G, then the path, then Return, 0.1–0.4 s, and Go to Folder flashes. Then press OKButton to open or save.
  • A Save picker keeps the typed name when it changes folder.

Why these files

  • Fits the app: types the app declares it opens. The picker's greyed rows can't be read, so this replaces them.
  • Context: same project or thread as the window behind the picker (Flix Files threads).
  • Drag memory: kinds you send to this app (Flix Files counts this already).
  • Picked here: what you chose in this app's picker before, learned when it closes. Open something twice and watch it move up. Stays on this Mac; Forget clears it.

Decide

  • Style: Shelf (beside), Ribbon (big cards above or below), or Tuck (icon rail that opens on hover).
  • Name: Flix Pick, Shortlist, Pickup, or your own.
  • Opening: both ⌥-click and double-click, or one of them?
  • Keyboard: a shortcut like ⌃1–⌃5 to pick a row while the picker has focus? This needs a global hot key, because the keys belong to the picker.
  • Per app: a "Not in this app" item in the panel's ⋯ menu.
