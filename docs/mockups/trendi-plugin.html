Trendi in DOKK interactive mockup · first real DOKK plugin

What this is. Trendi ships a DOKK plugin inside Trendi.app. Its tile sits in the plugin section, and its badge appears only while Trendi would nudge you. Clicking the tile opens the panel. Everything under the 40 pt header is Trendi's own SwiftUI, running in Trendi's process. Things to try:
  • Swipe a card: right to like, left for nope, up to Super Like. A quick flick is enough.
  • Click inside the panel, then use ← → ↑ and ⌘Z.
  • Right-click the tile for Trendi's four menu items, or right-click a card.
  • Switch Destination to a custom folder and Super Like a file to see the one-time grant.
  • Step through Polish T1 → T3.

Locked with Benn on 2026-10-23
  1. A plugin inside Trendi.app. Tested against the App Store / TestFlight build first. A native DOKK version is only the fallback if App Review says no.
  2. Its own tile in the plugin section, with Trendi's real app icon. DOKK's Downloads stack stays as it is.
  3. The badge counts only while Trendi would nudge you. Trendi's own nudges and menu bar count keep working unchanged.
  4. The full mini deck lives in the panel, with stats one tap away. The Statistics menu item opens the panel straight on Stats, through the new opensPanel flag on plugin menu items.
  5. Menu: Undo Last Decision, Review in Window, Snooze for Today, Statistics. Undo is listed only when there is something to undo, because plugin menu items have no disabled state.
  6. A custom Super Like folder is granted to the panel once, through the system open panel. The plugin can't read Trendi.app's bookmark.
For you to confirm in this mockup
  1. Panel size. Trendi asks for 380×370 content. DOKK adds the header and pointer, which makes the panel 380×420, the tallest DOKK allows. The card is 256×228, ¾ of Trendi's 340×430 card.
  2. Badge rule. The badge shows the waiting count when one of these holds:
    • 10 or more files are waiting;
    • the oldest file is at least 7 days old;
    • 24 h have passed since your last review.
    These are your Nudge settings. Trendi's 6 h quiet period after a notification doesn't hide the badge. While the panel is open the badge counts down with every decision, even after it drops under 10. When the panel closes, the rule is checked again. Snooze hides the badge until midnight.
  3. The undo chip. In the window, the undo toast sits at the bottom. In the panel it becomes a chip in the top bar, so it never covers the buttons. Its lifetime is the same 6 s.
  4. Keyboard. DOKK gives a plugin panel key focus on the first click, so a hint asks for that click. After it, ← → ↑ decide, ⌘Z undoes, and Space opens the file, as in Trendi's window. Esc closes the panel.
Polish tiers (each tier includes the ones before it; the mockup starts on T3)

T1 · Trendi feel at panel size

  • Card physics from DeckView: tilt about the base, a 3-card stack that closes up as you drag, a colour wash plus stamps, and predicted-end flicks.
  • Springs: cardReturn (0.34, 0.68), deckAdvance (0.42, 0.82), and cardExit (mass 0.55, k 90, c 16).
  • The Deck/Stats pill slides like a matched-geometry capsule. Stats counts up and its bars grow.
  • Buttons squash when pressed. The undo chip springs in. The empty state blooms. Keycaps fade in once the panel has focus.
  • Reduce Motion: no tilt, and every move is a 0.16 s ease. Reduce Transparency: solid surfaces.

T2 · Feedback you can feel

  • The tile badge ticks down with each decision.
  • Nope scatters the card into a puff of Trash-coloured particles as it leaves.
  • Super Like shrinks the card into the top-bar chip, so it visibly lands in its folder.
  • The top bar keeps a running "freed today" total that ticks up on Nope.

T3 · Delight

  • A confetti burst in the three decision colours when Downloads is clean.
  • Thumbnail parallax: the preview drifts against the drag, so the card reads as a physical object.
  • The stamp "thunks" when a drag crosses the commit threshold. It's the visual partner of the .levelChange haptic Trendi already plays.
