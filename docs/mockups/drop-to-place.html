Drop to Place: drag an app out of DOKK and drop it where its window should open

Drag an icon onto the desktop. Release it 64–180 pt from the dock and it unpins. Drag it farther than 180 pt and the icon unfolds into a ghost of the app's window. Its top-left corner sits at the pointer. Release to open the app there. A dashed outline shows where the window was last time; drop near it to snap back. Push into a screen edge to tile. Hold ⇧ while placing to draw a custom size, and press Esc to cancel. Running apps (dot) move their front window. Close a window with its red dot to "quit" an app, and DOKK remembers the spot.
