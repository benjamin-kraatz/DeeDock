DOKK Hub (DEE-121) mockup. Click the DOKK tile (first in the dock) to open it. Switch tabs with ⌘1 ⌘2 ⌘3. In Files, drag rows between the two panes, onto folders, the sidebar, or a tab. On the same disk that's a move; hold ⌥ or drop onto USB-Stick to copy, which shows the progress bar with pause and cancel. Hovering a folder during a drag opens it after a moment. Also try double-click, right-click, Space for Quick Look, ↑↓ ↩, ⌘T, and typing in the search field (Spotlight across the Mac). The pin button turns the panel into a normal window you can move and resize.
