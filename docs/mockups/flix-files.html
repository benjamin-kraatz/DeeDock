Which default?

Click one to try it in the scene above, or click the shortcut field and press your own. Ease is how quickly one hand can hit it without looking.

v1 scope

  • Files and folders used in the last 30 days, grouped Today / Yesterday / This Week / Earlier.
  • Kind chips, a time segment, Opened in and Where menus. Filters combine with the search text.
  • Bookmarks sit above the recents. They ignore the time filter (a bookmark never ages out) but follow search and kind.
  • Collections are colored groups. After ⌘D, the footer offers them in place, no dialog.
  • Hiding never touches the file. Settings lists hidden items to bring back.

Motion

  • Open: spring from 94 % scale, 10 pt above, 8 pt blur. Rows enter with the stagger above, first 12 only.
  • Close: 160 ms ease-in to 97 %, no spring; the panel gets out of the way.
  • Filter change: rows that stay slide to their new place; new rows fade up. The kind and time pills slide.
  • ⌘D: star pops with a small burst and the icon flies into the Bookmarks row.
  • Reduce Motion: every move becomes a 150 ms crossfade, no burst, no flight. Reduce Transparency: solid panel and menus.

Honest gaps to settle in the build

  • Folder recents need Finder to stamp kMDItemLastUsedDate on folders. Not verified yet.
  • Spotlight does not record which app opened a file. The Opened in filter needs a real source, or it ships as "Opens with" (the default app) and says so.
  • The panel takes keyboard focus when you summon it (that's the point), and gives it back to the previous app on Esc.
