DOKK 0.17.1, Build 54.

### Improvements

- The Approach Indicator now shows a ghost of the dock. Just before the activation zone, a faint monochrome outline fades in exactly where the dock will appear, with its glass and each tile at resting size. It starts under the pointer, spreads across the whole dock as you close in, and reaches full strength at the zone, so you can see both how far you still have to go and what is about to appear.
- Icons show as grayscale imprints whatever the icon style. The ghost is white over a light or accent glow and graphite over a shadow. Reduce Motion changes only its opacity, and Reduce Transparency draws crisp outlines without imprints.
- Turn it on or off in Settings › Behavior › Approach Indicator › "Show a ghost of the dock". It is on by default, can be set per display, and shows only while the approach glow is on.
