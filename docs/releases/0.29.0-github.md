DOKK 0.29.0, Build 82.

### New features

- New: Flix Files keeps what you opened, downloaded, saved and screenshotted, so you can drag it straight into the window in front: an upload form, a mail, a chat. It has its own tile in the dock. Click it or press ⌃⌥⌘F to open the panel. The tile shows the newest item, and dragging the tile carries that item.
- The panel groups items into Just Now, Today, Yesterday and Earlier. The sidebar filters by Opened, Downloaded, Saved, Flagged and kind, holds your Smart Filters, and takes drops on Favorites: a folder moves the files there, with Undo, and an app opens them.
- Fits this window: when the panel opens, Flix Files reads the window in front and suggests up to three files it asks for. When the window states limits such as “PDF only, max 5 MB”, Flix Files prepares a copy that fits. The original never changes.
- Search in your own words, such as “contract from last week” or “invoices over €1,000”. There are also Threads that group related work, captions and better names for files like `IMG_4821`, key facts such as due date and total, and Versions that collapse under the newest. Tidy sorts your Downloads folder in one step.
- All of this runs on your Mac, with Apple Intelligence and, when you set it up, Lookout. Only Ask Robi can send anything off the Mac, and only when you ask. Sensitive files such as IDs or bank details are blurred, and DOKK asks before you send one.
- New: Flix Pick. When an app shows an Open or Save window, DOKK puts a narrow rail beside it with the files that app most likely wants. Click selects the file in the window, and Option-click or double-click opens it right away. Beside a Save window it lists likely folders.
