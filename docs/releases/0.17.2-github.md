DOKK 0.17.2, Build 55.

### Improvements

- Window Peek now takes over a badge's banner. With the Line icon style, pointing at a badged tile shows a label with the count and the newest banner. Window Peek used to cover that label, and the banner was lost. Now the label flies on an arc into a strip on the dock side of the Peek panel: below the windows, or under the header on a top dock. The panel grows to fit, and a red edge marks where it lands.
- The strip shows the sender, the count, and up to two lines of the message. Clicking it opens the app and acknowledges the badge. If Peek opens before the label appears, the strip is already in the panel. Reduce Motion skips the flight, and Reduce Transparency drops the glow.
