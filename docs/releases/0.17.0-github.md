DOKK 0.17.0, Build 53.

### New

- The Approach Indicator shows where a hidden dock will appear. As the pointer nears the activation zone, a soft glow rises from the screen edge. It follows the pointer along the edge, surges just before the zone, and fades as the dock appears. Turn it on in Settings › Behavior › Approach Indicator › "Glow as the pointer approaches". It is off by default and shows only while "Automatically hide" is on.
- "Contrast with wallpaper" draws a light glow on a dark wallpaper and a shadow on a light one. "Accent color" uses the system accent. Both can be set per display. Reduce Motion changes only the glow's opacity, and Reduce Transparency draws a solid bar. The glow lets clicks through and never takes focus.
- With the Line icon style, a red ring around the glyph replaces the red corner dot. When a badge appears or its count rises, the ring draws in and the glyph plays its motion. Hovering brightens the ring, and the label adds the count and, when the Notification Feed is on, the app's newest banner.
- Clicking the tile or switching to the app acknowledges the badge: a white ring sweeps over the red and out again. An acknowledged badge stays quiet, even after a restart, until its count rises. The Notification Feed bell shows unread notifications with the same ring.
- Usage analytics count how many badges became new and how they were acknowledged. Badge text, app names, and banners are not sent.
- The compact Launcher now shows app suggestions. With an empty search field, a "Suggested" row of up to three apps sits above the grid, as in the full Launcher. A suggestion's context menu rates or hides it, and the arrow keys move between the suggestions and the grid.
- A full Trash has its own Line glyph with paper above the rim. Its motion drops a fresh paper ball in.

### Improvements

- Context menus open at the pointer. While a menu is open, its tile stays in place at full color, the rest of the dock recedes, and hover labels stay hidden, so you can tell which tile the menu belongs to.

### Fixes

- In the compact Launcher, the grid scrolls when the pointer is between tiles, and a click in a gap no longer closes the Launcher.
