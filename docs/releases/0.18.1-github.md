DOKK 0.18.1, Build 57.

### Improvements

- To help us decide which Line icons to draw next, usage analytics now count how many installed and pinned apps have one. For publicly available apps without an icon, such as apps that come with macOS, from the Mac App Store, or from Homebrew, the bundle ID is included. Other apps, such as your own builds or your company's tools, are only counted. DOKK checks shortly after launch, at most every six hours, and only while usage sharing is on. Settings › General › Privacy describes what is sent.
