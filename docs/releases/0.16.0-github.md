DOKK 0.16.0, Build 52.

### New

- Line icons move. Rest the pointer on a tile and its glyph plays a short motion once: the Trash lid lifts, the Finder face blinks, the Xcode hammer strikes, the Telegram plane darts forward, the Ghostty cursor blinks. More than 230 glyphs have a motion of their own that fits the app, and the rest draw themselves on or pop. Moving the keyboard selection onto a tile plays it too.
- A quick sweep along the dock leaves the glyphs still, and a Launcher result that appears already selected does not play. The dock, the Launcher, and the Open With list share the same motions.
- "Animate icons on hover" under Settings › Appearance › Icon style switches every motion off at once and keeps the colored glow. It is on by default, applies only to the Line style, and can be overridden per display. Reduce Motion keeps the glyphs still regardless.
- Waterfox has its own line glyph.
